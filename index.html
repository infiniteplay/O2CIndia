Order-to-Cash in India:
An Operational Reference

o2cindia.com is a documentation project by the team at Finifi. Our goal is to map the technical and operational realities of Order-to-Cash (O2C) within the Indian enterprise ecosystem.

We focus on the “last mile” of finance: the gap where ERP data meets bank statements, marketplace reports, and physical distribution reality.

The Structural Challenges of O2C in India

In high-volume Indian enterprises, the O2C cycle often breaks because systems aren’t built to talk to one another. Revenue doesn’t stall due to lack of sales; it stalls because the data required to settle those sales is fragmented.

Our observations on why O2C breaks:

  • The Channel Information Gap Sales data from Modern Trade (MT), General Trade (GT), and E-commerce (Quick Commerce/Marketplaces) arrive in non-standard formats that ERPs cannot natively ingest.
  • The Reconciliation Trap Banks provide credits, but often without the UTR or reference details needed to clear an open invoice.
  • The Deduction Maze Tax (TDS), schemes, and logistical credit notes create a “noisy” ledger where the cash received rarely matches the invoice value.
  • ERP Rigidity Traditional ERPs are excellent for recording history but often lack the flexibility to handle the real-time, high-frequency matching required for modern Indian commerce.

The Architecture of “System-Led” O2C

We believe that “good” finance operations are system-led, not person-led. Based on our work at Finifi, we’ve identified the architectural pillars of a mature O2C function:

  1. Automated Ingestion
    Directly fetching settlement reports from marketplaces and MT portals via APIs or automated scrapers.
  2. Algorithmic Matching
    Moving beyond manual Excel lookups to use fuzzy logic and UTR-matching algorithms that pair bank credits to invoices in real-time.
  3. Exception-Only Workflows
    Designing systems where finance teams only intervene when a dispute or short-payment occurs, rather than touching every transaction.
  4. ERP Synchronization
    Ensuring that once a match is found, the sub-ledger is updated automatically without manual data entry.
